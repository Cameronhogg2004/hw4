# Design — Problem 10

**Concept:** Campus Customs as a Fortnite-style **Item Shop in Yale blue**. Shopping feels like opening the daily shop in the game: rarity-coded loot tiles, a Battle Bus overhead, and a loot llama who runs the chat. Everything is drawn with HTML/CSS (no generated images).

| Area | What changed | Why it helps people stay and buy |
|---|---|---|
| **Fonts** | **Anton** (tall, heavy, skewed −7° like the in-game font) for headings, prices and buttons. **Nunito** (rounded, friendly) for body text. Both are bundled locally with `@fontsource`. | Bold display type makes prices and calls to action easy to read at a glance. The rounded body font keeps longer descriptions easy to read. |
| **Colours** | Storm-purple-to-Battle-Bus-blue sky, Yale-blue header and footer, **gold reserved for money and actions** (prices, "Enter the item shop", Send). Rarity colors by price tier: uncommon green, rare blue, epic purple, legendary orange. | Gold always means "price or next step", so the eye goes straight to buying. Rarity colors show the price tier before the shopper reads anything. |
| **Hierarchy** | Hero → trust strip (Officially licensed · 57 Broadway · Live stock) → **Featured** big tile + 4 → **Shop by squad** category tiles → footer. Gold announcement ticker across the top. | Value and trust come first, then the best products, then a simple way to browse. The ticker sells pickup and the chat without taking space. |
| **Product presentation** | Loot tiles: rotating light rays behind a framed photo, a rarity tag, color swatch dots, a gold skewed price tag, and **"Only N left" / "Sold out"** flags. The product page is a "locker" view with a spinning spotlight, a floating photo, color swatches, and **size buttons with stock meters**. | Shoppers can see color, price and stock without clicking. Low-stock flags add honest urgency, and stock meters answer "is my size available?" right away. |
| **Motion** | Battle Bus flies across the hero, the storm ring pulses, the llama bobs and blinks. Cards rise in one after another, lift and tilt on hover with a shine sweep. Daily-drops countdown to midnight. All motion turns off for shoppers who set reduced motion. | The motion makes the site fun to explore without slowing it down. The countdown and hover feedback give people a reason to keep browsing and come back tomorrow. |
| **Chat feel** | A gold llama launcher that hops, with a red "1" badge and a "Need your size? Ask me!" nudge. The chat is styled like in-game squad chat, with an online dot, llama avatars, bouncing typing dots and gold quick-reply chips. Results come back as mini cards plus a "Llama's picks" shelf on the page. | It feels like talking to a shop character, not a support widget, so more shoppers try it. Every answer leads to clickable products. |

**Files:** `frontend/src/index.css` (all styling), `components/BattleBus.tsx`, `components/Countdown.tsx`, `components/LootLlama.tsx`, `components/ProductCard.tsx`, `pages/Home.tsx`, `pages/ProductDetail.tsx`.
